# 00 Start Here

This is a free, structured knowledge base on growing a direct-to-consumer ecommerce brand. It covers buyer psychology, research, offers, ad creative, copy, landing pages, Meta and Google ads, retention, sourcing, finance and AI workflows. Curated by Josh Omosebi, Seba Agency.

Each note covers one idea and uses the same layout: **What it is**, **When to use it**, **How (steps)**, **Example**, **Common mistakes** and **Related**. Notes are tagged by stage:

- **beginner**: you are launching or have not found a reliable winner yet.
- **intermediate**: you have a product that sells and want consistent, profitable testing.
- **scaling**: you are spending meaningfully every day and fighting fatigue, rising costs and cash-flow pressure.

## How to use it

1. Find your section in the map below and open its **Map** note. Each map lists every note in that section with a one-line description.
2. Follow the **Related** links at the bottom of each note. They lead to the next idea you will need.
3. Unfamiliar term? Check the glossary notes at the bottom of this page.

## Suggested reading paths

**New brand, no winners yet.** Start with the foundations of why ads work. Then learn how to research your customer before you write a word. Next, shape an offer and angles worth testing. After that, learn how hooks and scripts are built, and how to run a simple, disciplined first test.

**Selling, but inconsistent.** Tighten the page people land on, learn to read your numbers, and build an iteration routine around what is already working.
→ [[06 Landing Pages, CRO & Funnels - Map]] → [[11 Finance & Unit Economics - Map]] → [[05 Copywriting & Storytelling - Map]]

**Scaling.** Add channels and retention, keep cash flow healthy, and use AI to produce more creative without losing quality.
→ [[08 Google Ads - Map]] → [[09 Email, SMS & Retention - Map]] → [[10 Product Research & Sourcing - Map]] → [[12 AI Workflows for Ecom - Map]]

## A note on using this material

Everything here is general education, not legal, financial or medical advice. Numbers in examples are illustrative. Benchmarks are rules of thumb and vary by product, price point and market. Platform features change often, so check current platform rules and local advertising law before you act.
