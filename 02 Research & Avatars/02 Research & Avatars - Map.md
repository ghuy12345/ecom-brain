# 02 Research & Avatars - Map

Research is where every good ad, page and offer starts. This section walks from understanding what you sell, through the places to find what your market wants and believes, to turning that research into specific avatars you can write for. A sensible order for beginners: product understanding, then the research document, then a few research sources, then avatars. Survey notes are for brands with a large enough customer list.
