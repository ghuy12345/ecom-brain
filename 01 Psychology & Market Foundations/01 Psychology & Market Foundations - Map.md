# 01 Psychology & Market Foundations - Map

This section covers why people buy and how a market's state shapes what you should say: how aware and how skeptical prospects are, which desires you can channel, how emotion and belief decide whether a claim lands, and the working loop that turns those ideas into results. Start with awareness, sophistication and mass desire if you are new; the rest builds on them.
