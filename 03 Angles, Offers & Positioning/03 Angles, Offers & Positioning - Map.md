# 03 Angles, Offers & Positioning - Map

This section turns research into reasons to buy. Angles decide what each ad says and to whom; mechanisms and authority make those claims believable; positioning decides why anyone should pick your brand over the alternatives; and offers decide how compelling the deal is once they arrive. If you are new, read the angle basics first, then jump to the offer notes, since the offer is usually the fastest early win.

- [[Deriving Ad Angles From Sub-Avatars]] — finding the gap between what a sub-avatar wants and what they already do, to pull angles and direct hooks out of each sub-avatar.
- [[One Avatar, One Desire, One Message]] — why every ad and page should carry a single reason to buy for a single person.
- [[Ad Angle Types by Appeal Category]] — a palette of angle types grouped by appeal, with the awareness and sophistication stages each fits.

- [[New Information Angles]] — teaching the buyer a true, verified fact, screened for congruence and emotional intensity.
