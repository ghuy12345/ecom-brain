# Sale-Period Campaign Structure

## What it is
In normal weeks, separate retargeting rarely pays because a broad campaign already finds warm buyers. Real limited-time sales are the exception: people who already know you respond strongly to a clear deadline and offer, and you do not mind whether a buyer is new or returning. A sale campaign is a temporary, separate campaign built to harvest that intent, running alongside your evergreen campaigns rather than replacing them.

## When to use it
- Genuine time-limited events: Black Friday and Cyber Monday, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Memorial Day, a brand anniversary sale.
- Never for fake "ends tonight" sales that run forever.
- A rolling series of sales with no return to normal pricing does not need a sale campaign; just update your evergreen ads.

## How (steps)
1. **Create one campaign-budget campaign with three ad sets:**
   - **Broad:** age, gender and country only.
   - **Warm:** people who engaged with your Facebook page or Instagram account, plus anyone who watched 95% of a video, all within 60 days. Include every video, ad or organic, that has passed about a thousand views.
   - **Hot:** site visitors and people who viewed content, added to cart or started checkout in the past 90 days. Remove the hot audiences from the warm ad set to prevent overlap.
2. **Leave allocation to the campaign budget.** It does not matter whether money goes to broad or to retargeting. Should warm or hot underperform, switch them off or cap them low; the right mix differs by brand and shifts during the sale.
3. **Use blunt, most-aware creative.** Put sale banners over your best existing videos and images by duplicating and badging them (never switch off the evergreen original to do it), plus simple product images that state the offer. Add a few benefit-plus-offer ads for colder people and gifting creator videos where relevant. The offer should be readable at a glance, and the first line of copy should state the offer and the end date.
4. **Keep evergreen running.** Mixing sale and evergreen ads in one campaign makes spend swing between them and disrupts learning. Keep evergreen campaigns live and only update headlines or announcement-bar messaging in them.
5. **Build a sale theme** for the store (sale branding, imagery, offer display) and display the savings in the cart and at checkout, while the evergreen site experience remains coherent.
6. **Schedule the shutdown.** In Ads Manager Rules, create a custom rule scoped to the sale campaign with the action "turn off" and the condition Time, Current time, is greater than your end date and time. Rules are checked roughly every 30 minutes, so leave some margin when the end time matters. Test by setting a past time and checking it fires.
7. **Scale it intraday** with the rules in [[Intraday Budget Scaling for Sale Periods]].

## Launch timing and budget
**Timing.** There is a choice. Launching the main sale early (early November for BFCM) gives two to three weeks of delivery data, lets you test sale creative with the real offer live, and matches early-shopping customers. Launching late (around the weekend before Black Friday or the start of that week) keeps the offer feeling special and protects margin. Brands with a strong, distinct offer and the creative volume to sustain it lean early; newer brands or those worried about discount fatigue lean later. Either way, do not start a sale campaign in September, and upload sale ads several days early to clear review.

**Budget.** There is no formula. Start from risk tolerance: anywhere from a few hundred dollars a day to roughly a tenth of total spend for a first run. Half of daily spend is about the most you would ever commit, and only with high confidence. Start cautiously; a working sale campaign scales fast.

## Example
Say a kitchenware brand at $4,000/day launches its BFCM sale campaign the Monday before Black Friday at $400/day: broad, warm and hot ad sets, with badged versions of its five top evergreen ads and three offer statics. Hot audiences take 30% of spend at a strong return, warm underperforms and is capped at $50/day. An automated rule switches the campaign off at 11:59pm on Cyber Monday plus a short buffer.

## Common mistakes
- Turning evergreen campaigns off for the sale.
- Reusing evergreen post IDs inside the sale campaign instead of fresh badged duplicates.
- Spamming many separate sale campaigns.
- Forgetting to switch the sale off on time.
- Launching a brand-new store or ad account during sale week, when CPMs are at their highest.
