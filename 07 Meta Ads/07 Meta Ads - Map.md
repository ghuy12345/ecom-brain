# 07 Meta Ads - Map

This section covers running Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads for an ecommerce brand: setting up the account, keeping the structure simple, testing creative, reading metrics honestly, scaling budgets, diagnosing problems, running sale periods and expanding beyond your first market and channel. A recurring theme: below very high spend, creative, offers and pages matter far more than structure. If you are new, start with setup, then [[Creative Over Media-Buying Structure]] and [[Creative Testing Campaign Setup]].
