# 08 Google Ads - Map

Google Ads for ecommerce is mostly a Shopping game: product listings, a clean feed, consolidated campaigns and honest measurement. Start with structure and the roadmap, then work through campaign types, bidding and the feed. Use the diagnosis notes when performance changes, and the tracking notes to make sure Google learns from good data.
